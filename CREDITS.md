## Instrument audio

**Philharmonia Orchestra sound samples** — https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/
Specially recorded by members of the Philharmonia. Published terms (verbatim, retrieved 2026-08):
> "Our library of sound samples has been specially recorded by members of the
> Philharmonia, and is free to use... You can use these samples as you wish,
> including releasing them as part of a commercial work. The only restriction is
> that they must not be sold or made available as-is, i.e. as a sampler instrument."

Used here: short single-note/phrase recordings bundled inside this game (a work),
not redistributed as a standalone sample library. Source files: violin, guitar, cello,
double bass, flute, clarinet, saxophone, bassoon, trumpet, trombone, French horn, tuba,
snare drum, tom-toms, triangle, woodblock, bass drum, banana shaker (played as
"Shaker Eggs").

**Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra** (harp, timpani, xylophone) — by Mattias Westlund,
licensed CC-BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via the peastman/sso
GitHub mirror. Used here: `harp-c4.wav`, `harp-a5.wav`, `timpani-f-lh-c2.wav`,
`xylophone-c#4.wav`, converted to mono MP3.
